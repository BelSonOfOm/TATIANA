\chapter{The V2 Reformulation}\label{ch:v2}

\section{What changed, and why the book must change with it}

This book was written for \textbf{V0}: an architecture in which a C\raisebox{0.2ex}{\small ++}
dispatcher called out to typed \emph{organs} --- Search, Reason, Context,
Verify --- one of which was a remote language model. Under that framing the
mathematics of this book was a \emph{library}: the dispatcher decided when
to run the growth law, when to build a cone, when to fire the adjunction.
The sheaf was something the code consulted.

V0 is retired. The reason is not that its mathematics failed --- most of it
survives, and Part~\ref{ch:limits} records exactly which parts did fail ---
but that the architecture was the wrong shape for the thing it was built to
be. What was built was a complex of organs wrapped around a borrowed brain.
What is wanted is a single kernel that, meeting a problem, mutates, grows and
reproduces itself to cover it, while remaining provably the same kernel
throughout.

\begin{intu}
\Intu The difference is who is in charge. In V0, the mathematics was a set of
tools and a program decided when to pick up each one. In V2, there is no
program outside the mathematics: the kernel's identity \emph{is} ``the thing
that keeps applying its own laws to itself.'' Nothing conducts it.
\end{intu}

\begin{sourcebox}\small\textsf{\textbf{Source.}}
\texttt{DOCS/TATIANA\_LOGBOOK.md}~\S8 records the decision;
\texttt{DOCS/TATIANA\_V1\_IDENTITY\_MAP.md} is the binding statement of what
V2 is and is the document this book is now being rewritten to match;

\section{The commitment}

Everything in this book is now answerable to one sentence.

\begin{quote}\itshape
\textsc{tatiana} is a cellular sheaf on a stratified $2$-complex whose
section relaxes, whose restriction maps mold, and whose complex grows and
prunes --- under a two-phase law that conserves a stated invariant exactly
while awake and projectively while offline.
\end{quote}

Read the clauses as constraints. Each one forbids something, and each
forbidding is a live constraint on the chapters that follow.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{p{0.42\textwidth}p{0.5\textwidth}}
\toprule
\textbf{clause} & \textbf{forbids} \\
\midrule
cellular sheaf on a \textbf{2-complex} & dropping to a graph \\
section \textbf{relaxes} & treating $s$ as stored data rather than a dynamical variable \\
restriction maps \textbf{mold} & freezing $\Fs$, which makes the sheaf decorative \\
complex \textbf{grows and prunes} & growth-only architectures that inflate without bound \\
\textbf{two-phase} & trading all costs against each other on every tick \\
\textbf{stated invariant} & ``identity'' as a vibe rather than a number that can be printed \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\section{What \textsc{tatiana} is not}\label{sec:isnot}

Each of these was a live position in this project at some point, which is why
each is recorded rather than assumed.

\begin{enumerate}
  \item \textbf{Not a language-model wrapper.} No router, no
    retrieval-augmented arm, no ``memory layer for a chat model.'' The
    language model is never the thing that reasons. Two roles remain
    structurally necessary --- an \emph{encoder} producing the observation
    $o_t$, and a \emph{verification lift} --- and V2's position on them is
    given in \S\ref{sec:encoder-position}: they are required, they are not to
    be filled by plugging in a pretrained model, and their own architecture
    is an open research question.
  \item \textbf{Not a knowledge graph.} A knowledge graph has no dynamics and
    no obstruction theory. If a proposed feature would work identically on a
    labelled graph, it does not belong here.
  \item \textbf{Not a cognitive-science model.} Mechanisms are borrowed from
    neuroscience as design constraints. No claim is made to explain brains,
    and nothing here should be evaluated against neural data.
  \item \textbf{Not the Free Energy Principle.} The two-phase objective
    contains structural and stability terms that are this project's
    construction. Calling it free energy imports authority that has not been
    earned. It is called the \emph{maintenance cost} and nothing else.
  \item \textbf{Not a system that must be conscious, agentic or ``alive.''}
    Reproduction is a morphism. No further reading is claimed.
\end{enumerate}

\section{The six structural changes}

These are the changes that force a rewrite rather than an edit.

\paragraph{1. One kernel, not a complex of organs.} The coarse/fine
stratification in Chapter~\ref{ch:complex} was justified by organs being real,
separate subsystems. With one kernel that justification is gone. Whether the
stratification survives at all --- and if so, on what new ground --- is a
question Part~\ref{ch:complex} must now answer rather than assume.

\paragraph{2. Identity is a number, and it is the centre of the book.} V0 had
no invariant. V2's is
\[
  \mathrm{K}(\mathbb{K}) \;=\; \big(\dim \Fs_{\mathbb{K}}(\sigma)\big)_{\sigma \in \CC_{\mathbb{K}}},
\]
the dimension vector over the crystallized store, exactly conserved by
molding and consolidation and changed only by growth, and then by a derived
amount. Identity is carried by $\mathbb{K}$; the working complex $W$ may be
destroyed at the end of every session. This has no chapter in the V0 book and
needs one at the front, not the back.

\paragraph{3. The dynamics are two-phase, not a single objective.}
\textsc{tatiana} does not minimise one functional every tick. It alternates:
a \textbf{wake} phase that discharges discord by the Hodge routing law, and a
\textbf{sleep} phase that renormalizes and prunes. Cost lives in sleep and
nowhere else. Growth adds during wake, pruning removes during sleep, and they
never bid against each other inside one tick.

\begin{hon}
\Hon A six-term $\lambda$-weighted energy functional was proposed for this
role and \textbf{refused}. The refusal is load-bearing, not stylistic: a
second exchange rate is evidence that the two-phase separation has failed.
Chapter~\ref{ch:twophase} states the one exchange rate that is allowed and
Part~\ref{ch:limits} records why the alternative was rejected. Do not
reintroduce it.
\end{hon}

\paragraph{4. Growth has an address problem, and it is now load-bearing.}
Proposition~8.2 --- $[\cob x]=0$ in $\Hone$ unconditionally, so a coboundary
can never name a gluing failure --- was, in V0, one blocked mechanism among
several. With no organ complex to lean on, a kernel that cannot address its
own growth has no growth at all. The fix requires a genuinely \emph{measured}
$1$-cochain that is not the coboundary of any global state, and the harmonic
component of its Hodge decomposition is the address. Under V0 that cochain
came from organs disagreeing with each other. V2 has no organs, so where it
comes from is open.

\paragraph{5. The $2$-cells are load-bearing, not decoration.} On a graph,
$\cob^1\cob^0=0$ is vacuous: every assignment of stalks and maps is a sheaf,
molding is unconstrained, and nothing intrinsic can ever stall. On a
$2$-complex it is a real algebraic condition. The triangles are what make
molding \emph{obstructible}, hence what make growth \emph{derivable}. Every
published negative result about learned sheaf Laplacians concerns graphs, and
this is the defence --- which means it must be stated as a theorem and not as
a hope.

\paragraph{6. Reproduction enters as a morphism.} The adjoint pair already in
the mathematics is the candidate: $\iota^*$ instantiates a subcomplex,
$\iota_!$ writes back only what was touched, and $\iota^*\iota_! \cong \Id$
guarantees the round trip does not corrupt the fragment. An offspring is a
downward-closed subcomplex with its pullback sheaf, carrying a sub-invariant.
No claim is made that it is viable. This is the weakest part of the design and
is placed last for that reason.

\section{Where the language model is, and is not}\label{sec:encoder-position}

\begin{hon}
\Hon The reasoner is never a language model. That is settled and is not
reopened anywhere in this book.

Two roles remain, and pretending otherwise would be dishonest: something must
turn raw input into an observation $o_t$ that the sheaf can act on (an
\emph{encoder}), and something must discharge the verification trichotomy
(a \emph{lift}). V2's position is narrower than V0's and is stated here so it
cannot drift: both roles are required; neither is to be satisfied by wrapping
a pretrained, externally-hosted model; and the architecture of each is an
\emph{open research question} to be derived from this project's own
mathematics the way every other component was. It is possible that what is
derived ends up structurally resembling machinery that also appears in
language models. If so, that must be an earned conclusion with a stated
derivation --- never a default, and never an import.
\end{hon}

\section{How the rewrite proceeds}

Every chapter that follows carries a \textbf{work plan} box and a
\textbf{disposition}:

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{ll}
\toprule
\Keep & The mathematics stands. Reframing only, if anything. \\
\Recast & The result survives but its justification or its role changes. \\
\Retire & Belongs to V0's architecture and does not survive the pivot. \\
\NewV & No V0 counterpart. Must be written from scratch. \\
\bottomrule
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

The work plan states the goal, the raw material, the ordered steps, and the
condition under which the section is finished. It is an instruction, not
commentary: when a section is written, its work plan is deleted and replaced
by the prose it asked for. A chapter with a work plan still in it is a chapter
that is not done.

\begin{hon}
\Hon Two rules govern the order of work, both earned the hard way in V0 and
both recorded in \texttt{MATH\_TOOLBOX.md}~Part~IX.

\emph{Write the falsifier before the implementation.} Every construction that
claims to be measurable states, before it is built, what result would refute
it. Building an instrument and a result at the same time is how V0 produced
four confident wrong answers in one week.

\emph{New mathematics earns its place only if it changes a number a program
prints, or deletes a decision a human was making by hand.} A construction that
does neither is decoration, however correct.
\end{hon}
