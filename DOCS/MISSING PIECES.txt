2. Can you drop this into Gemini Anti-Gravity?

Yes—but with an important qualification.

Gemini can absolutely implement the architecture that's described. The document is detailed enough that it should understand the intended modules, interfaces, and workflow.

However, if your goal is:

"Build the definitive mathematical operating system"

then I would not stop here.

I would create what software architects call an Architecture Constitution.

Think of it as the OS's laws.

For example:

Core Philosophy — why the OS exists and what it optimizes.
Non-Negotiable Invariants — sandbox is opt-in, planner decides goals, quality is never sacrificed for cost, etc.
Subsystem Contracts — every module's inputs, outputs, and responsibilities.
Research Foundations — why each design choice exists and the papers behind it.
Implementation Roadmap — phased development.
