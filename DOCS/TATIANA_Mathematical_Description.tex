\begin{abstract}
\noindent
We give a complete, implementation-free mathematical description of the cognitive
architecture called \textsc{tatiana} (its state object is the \emph{Modular Operating
System}, \textsc{mos}). The organising object is a \emph{cognitive state space}
$\M=(\CC,\Fs,s)$: a two-level (stratified) simplicial complex $\CC$, a cellular sheaf
$\Fs$ assigning geometric data to each piece of $\CC$, and a global section $s$
representing the system's current coherent state. The architecture wraps a fixed
large language model (LLM) used as a black-box reasoning oracle; its mathematical
content is not the reasoning itself but the \emph{organisation, measurement, and
evolution} of whole reasoning acts. We define every object from first principles,
derive the two quantities the system actually computes at every step---the sheaf
Laplacian discord $\omega$ and its normalised, provably bounded form
$\rho\in[0,1]$---and record every ``minute computation'' the engine performs: the
precision-weighted Gaussian coarse-graining $\pi_v$, the Bures--Wasserstein concept
distance, the Woodbury concept-fusion, the Anderson--Morley normaliser, the
cohomological diagnostics $\Hzero,\Hone,b_0$, and the growth operators
$\{\text{bind},\text{collapse},\text{split},\text{merge}\}$ together with the
operator-algebra growth axis $\Dop(t)\!\to\!\Dop(t+1)$. Throughout, boxed
\emph{claims-and-non-claims} remarks state precisely what each construction does and
does not establish; self-honesty is the single invariant the whole design exists to
protect.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction: the object and the thesis}

\subsection{What is being described}

\begin{intu}\Intu
Picture a research assistant that is not one mind but a small society of specialist
organs---one that remembers, one that searches, one that checks, one that reasons.
\textsc{mos} is the mathematics of \emph{that society}: who is bound to whom, whether
their beliefs agree, by how much they disagree and exactly where, and how the society
rewires itself as it works. The individual reasoning is done by an external LLM; the
mathematics here governs the \emph{society}, not the neurons.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Cognitive state space]\label{def:M}
A \emph{cognitive state space} is a triple $\M=(\CC,\Fs,s)$ where
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
  \item $\CC$ is a stratified (two-level) finite simplicial complex (Section~\ref{sec:complex});
  \item $\Fs$ is a cellular sheaf of finite-dimensional real inner-product spaces on $\CC$ (Section~\ref{sec:sheaf});
  \item $s$ is a distinguished $0$-cochain of $\Fs$, the \emph{current state}, whose
        coherence is measured by the sheaf Laplacian (Section~\ref{sec:coherence}).
\end{enumerate}
The space evolves in discrete time through structural operators drawn from an
operator algebra $\Dop$ that is \emph{itself} allowed to grow (Section~\ref{sec:growth}).
\end{definition}

\subsection{The thesis, stated plainly}

The architecture makes one honest bet and refuses one seductive overclaim.

\begin{hon}\Hon
\textbf{The bet (defensible):} a persistent, self-checking, self-restructuring
\emph{organiser} wrapped around a fixed LLM can, over time, produce better-grounded
and internally-consistent research artifacts than the raw LLM, through
\emph{measured cross-organ error-correction} and \emph{accumulation}. The advantage
is \emph{architectural}, expressed as operations on the single object $\M$.
\textbf{The refusal:} no simplex proves a theorem and no Laplacian integrates a
differential form. The per-inference reasoning quality is bounded by the LLM. The
mathematics here does not raise that ceiling; it organises, measures, and
error-corrects \emph{around} it. Section~\ref{sec:limits} makes this boundary precise
and states where it can, and cannot, be pushed.
\end{hon}

\section{The base complex \texorpdfstring{$\CC$}{C}}\label{sec:complex}
% =====================================================================

\subsection{Simplicial complexes and \texorpdfstring{$n$}{n}-ary binding}

\begin{intu}\Intu
A graph records \emph{pairwise} facts (``$A$ relates to $B$''). But a theorem that
depends on three hypotheses is \emph{one} three-way fact, not three separate pairs.
A simplicial complex is the smallest honest language for facts of any arity: a filled
triangle is a genuine three-way binding, not three edges.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Abstract simplicial complex]
Let $V$ be a finite vertex set. An \emph{abstract simplicial complex} $\CC$ on $V$ is
a family of nonempty subsets of $V$ (the \emph{simplices}) that is
\emph{downward closed}: if $\sigma\in\CC$ and $\emptyset\neq\tau\subseteq\sigma$ then
$\tau\in\CC$. A simplex of cardinality $k+1$ is a \emph{$k$-simplex}; its
$(k-1)$-subsets are its \emph{faces}. Thus $0$-simplices are vertices, $1$-simplices
edges, $2$-simplices filled triangles, and so on.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Simplices are $n$-ary relations]\label{rem:nary}
A $k$-simplex $\{v_0,\dots,v_k\}$ models a single $(k{+}1)$-ary binding among its
vertices. In the engine this is exactly the stored object
\texttt{insert\_n\_ary\_relation}: a fact with $k{+}1$ constituents is one simplex,
and downward closure is the requirement that every sub-collection of a jointly-bound
fact is itself recorded. This earns the later use of $b_1$ (Section~\ref{sec:cohom}):
a $1$-cycle is a real structural hole only because a filled $2$-simplex genuinely
means ``these three are jointly bound,'' not merely pairwise.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The two-level stratification}

\begin{intu}\Intu
The society has two scales. Zoom out and the vertices are whole \emph{organs}
(Search, Memory, Reasoning, Verify, $\dots$). Zoom into one organ and it is itself a
little complex whose vertices are \emph{concepts}. The same geometry runs at both
scales; the two are glued by a coarse-graining map (Section~\ref{sec:piv}).
\end{intu}

\begin{construction}[The stratified complex]\label{con:strat}
$\CC$ carries two strata.
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item The \emph{coarse complex} $\Kc$ has as vertices the cognitive organs
        \[
        \mathcal{V}(\Kc)=\{\textsc{Planner},\ \textsc{Search},\
        \textsc{Canonicalizer},\ \textsc{Curator},\ \textsc{WorkingMemory},\
        \textsc{Reasoning},\ \textsc{Verify}\}.
        \]
        A simplex $\sigma\in\Kc$ is a \emph{bound coalition} of organs acting as one
        unit; it carries a time-dependent \emph{coupling weight}
        $w(\sigma,t)\in[0,1]$.
  \item For each organ $v$, a \emph{fine complex} $\CC_v$ has concepts as vertices and
        $n$-ary conceptual bindings as simplices; this is the organ's internal
        knowledge.
\end{itemize}
The stratification resolves an early ambiguity (``are the vertices concepts or
subsystems?'') by answering \emph{both}, at two levels.
\end{construction}

\begin{remark}[Two structures on the same vertices]
A logical prerequisite chain is \emph{directed} ($A$ is needed for $B$); a simplicial
complex is undirected. The engine therefore keeps two relations on the same vertices:
a directed poset for logical dependency and the (undirected) simplices for $n$-ary
co-binding. They are never conflated.
\end{remark}

% =====================================================================
\section{The sheaf \texorpdfstring{$\Fs$}{F}}\label{sec:sheaf}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
On top of the shape we lay \emph{data}. Each organ holds a vector describing what it
currently believes; each binding holds a space in which two organs' beliefs are meant
to be compared. A sheaf is exactly this: local data on every piece, plus rules for
comparing across pieces. A ``coherent thought'' is a choice of data that agrees
everywhere it is compared.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Cellular sheaf, after Curry and Hansen--Ghrist]\label{def:sheaf}
A \emph{cellular sheaf} $\Fs$ of real inner-product spaces on $\CC$ assigns
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
  \item to each cell $\sigma$ a finite-dimensional inner-product space
        $\Fs(\sigma)$, the \emph{stalk};
  \item to each incident face pair $\sigma\triang\tau$ (i.e. $\sigma$ a face of
        $\tau$) a linear \emph{restriction map}
        $\rest{\sigma\triang\tau}:\Fs(\sigma)\to\Fs(\tau)$,
\end{enumerate}
compatibly on chains of incidences. In this project all vertex and edge stalks are
$\Rd$ with $d=384$ (the embedding dimension, Section~\ref{sec:semantic}); the
restriction maps of the coarse sheaf are indexed $\rest{v\triang e}$ for a vertex $v$
on edge $e$.
\end{definition}

\begin{definition}[Cochains and sections]
A \emph{$0$-cochain} is a choice $x=(x_v)_{v}$ with $x_v\in\Fs(v)$; write
$C^0(\CC;\Fs)=\bigoplus_v \Fs(v)$. Likewise $C^1(\CC;\Fs)=\bigoplus_e \Fs(e)$. A
\emph{global section} is a $0$-cochain whose organ beliefs agree under every
restriction (Definition~\ref{def:coboundary}). The current state $s$ of
Definition~\ref{def:M} is a $0$-cochain; it is a genuine ``thought'' precisely when it
is a global section.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[The stalk data is a real embedding, never a placeholder]\label{rem:embed}
Stalk vectors are sentence-embeddings from a fixed local encoder (dimension
$d=384$). Two design invariants are enforced as \emph{type-level} facts, because their
violation silently corrupts every downstream distance: \emph{(i)} dimensions never
truncate or pad---a mismatch raises; \emph{(ii)} an idle organ has \emph{no} stalk
value (a formal $\bot$), never the zero vector, because $0\in\Rd$ is a real point that
would drag every distance toward the origin and fabricate a measurement. These are the
sheaf-theoretic content of ``do not fake data.''
\end{remark}

\section{Coherence: the sheaf Laplacian and the score \texorpdfstring{$\rho$}{rho}}\label{sec:coherence}
% =====================================================================

This section derives the two numbers the engine computes on \emph{every} tick.

\subsection{Coboundary and Laplacian}

\begin{definition}[Coboundary]\label{def:coboundary}
Fix an arbitrary orientation of each edge $e=\{u,v\}$. The \emph{coboundary}
$\delta:C^0(\CC;\Fs)\to C^1(\CC;\Fs)$ acts edgewise by
\begin{equation}\label{eq:coboundary}
(\delta x)_e \;=\; \rest{u\triang e}(x_u)\;-\;\rest{v\triang e}(x_v)\ \in\ \Fs(e).
\end{equation}
A $0$-cochain $x$ is a \emph{global section} iff $\delta x=0$, i.e. every bound pair
agrees after mapping into the shared comparison space $\Fs(e)$.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[The organ contract must be ANTISYMMETRIC]\label{rem:antisym}
Proposition~\ref{prop:H0} makes the coboundary $\delta x$ useless as a growth signal:
it is exact by construction, so $[\delta x]=0$ in $H^1$ for \emph{every} state, every
sheaf, and every choice of restriction maps. Detecting an obstruction requires a
genuinely \emph{measured} $1$-cochain $\eta$---organs emitting pairwise judgements---
rather than $\delta$ of any global object.

The fixing of an orientation in Definition~\ref{def:coboundary} is then not a
bookkeeping convenience but a \textbf{constraint on what may be elicited}. A
$1$-cochain satisfies $\eta_{(v,u)}=-\eta_{(u,v)}$, so any \emph{symmetric} quantity---
an ``agreement score'', a similarity, a shared confidence---antisymmetrises to
$\eta\equiv 0$ and carries no information whatsoever into the Hodge decomposition. The
elicitation contract must therefore return, per pair, a shared sub-claim and two
\emph{signed pushes}:
\[
  \bigl(\text{sub-claim},\ p_u,\ p_v,\ c\bigr),\qquad
  \eta_{(u,v)} \;=\; p_v-p_u ,\qquad p_u,p_v\in[-1,1],\ c\in[0,1],
\]
which is antisymmetric by construction rather than by assumption, with the confidence
$c$ routed to $\nu$ and thence to $\pi_e$ per Remark~\ref{rem:tower}. The shared
sub-claim is load-bearing: without a common proposition the two pushes are not answers
to the same question, and no sign convention can repair that afterwards.
\end{remark}

\begin{definition}[Sheaf Laplacian and discord]\label{def:laplacian}
The (degree-$0$) \emph{sheaf Laplacian} is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:laplacian}
L \;=\; \delta^{\top}\delta \;:\; C^0\to C^0 ,
\end{equation}
a symmetric positive-semidefinite operator. The \emph{discord} of a state $x$ is the
Dirichlet energy
\begin{equation}\label{eq:omega}
\omega(x)\;=\;x^{\top}Lx\;=\;\norm{\delta x}^2
\;=\;\sum_{e=\{u,v\}}\ \norm{\rest{u\triang e}(x_u)-\rest{v\triang e}(x_v)}^{2}\ \ge 0 .
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[A coherent thought is a kernel element]\label{prop:H0}
$\omega(x)=0 \iff \delta x = 0 \iff x\in\ker L$. Moreover the space of global
sections is exactly the zeroth sheaf cohomology,
\begin{equation}
\Hzero(\CC;\Fs)\;=\;\ker\delta\;=\;\ker L .
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\omega(x)=\norm{\delta x}^2=0\iff \delta x=0$ since $\norm{\cdot}$ is a norm. As
$L=\delta^\top\delta$, $Lx=0\Rightarrow x^\top Lx=\norm{\delta x}^2=0\Rightarrow\delta
x=0$; conversely $\delta x=0\Rightarrow Lx=0$. Finally $\Hzero=\ker\delta^{0}$ because
there are no $(-1)$-cochains to quotient by.
\end{proof}

\begin{intu}\Intu
$\omega$ is a single number: the total squared disagreement across all bindings. It is
zero exactly when the society holds one coherent thought. And ``a coherent thought'' is
now a precise mathematical object---a vector in the kernel of a matrix---not a mood.
\end{intu}

\subsection{The identity-restriction baseline}

\begin{proposition}[Graph-Laplacian form]\label{prop:identity}
If every $\rest{v\triang e}=\mathrm{Id}_{\Rd}$, then
$\omega(x)=\sum_{e=\{u,v\}}\norm{x_u-x_v}^2$ and
$L=L_\Kc\otimes I_d$, where $L_\Kc$ is the ordinary combinatorial graph Laplacian of
the coarse $1$-skeleton. Consequently the spectrum of $L$ is that of $L_\Kc$ with each
eigenvalue repeated $d$ times.
\end{proposition}

\begin{hon}\Hon
The current engine uses identity restriction maps (``do bound organs agree in
embedding space?''). This is honest and cheap but it is the \emph{crudest} sheaf: with
a connected $1$-skeleton, $\ker L$ is the diagonal $\{x:x_u=x_v\ \forall u,v\}$, so the
only coherent states are \emph{consensus}, and reconciliation is plain averaging. The
``quality of a connection'' lives in \emph{non-trivial} restriction maps; promoting
them (learned $\rest{v\triang e}$, e.g. by closed-form Procrustes/CCA alignment) is the
identified route from a diagnostic sheaf to a computational one. This is stated as a
direction, not as an implemented result (Section~\ref{sec:limits}).
\end{hon}

\subsection{The normalised score \texorpdfstring{$\rho\in[0,1]$}{rho in [0,1]} and its bound}

\begin{intu}\Intu
$\omega$ has no natural scale---bigger complexes have bigger $\omega$. We want a
dimensionless thermometer in $[0,1]$: $0=$ perfect agreement, $1=$ maximal possible
disagreement. Crucially we get it \emph{without} an eigendecomposition, using a
classical bound on the largest Laplacian eigenvalue.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Coherence score]\label{def:rho}
Let $X\in\R^{|V|\times d}$ stack the active stalks as rows, with Frobenius norm
$\norm{X}_F$. With the \emph{Anderson--Morley normaliser}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:AM}
B \;=\; \max_{e=\{u,v\}\in E}\big(\deg u+\deg v\big),
\end{equation}
the \emph{coherence score} (in the identity-restriction regime) is
\begin{equation}\label{eq:rho}
\boxed{\ \rho \;=\; \dfrac{\omega}{B\,\norm{X}_F^{2}}\ }\qquad\in[0,1].
\end{equation}
When $E=\emptyset$ or $\norm{X}_F=0$, $\rho$ is \emph{undefined} ($\bot$, ``unknown''),
never $0$ and never $1$.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[$\rho\in[0,1]$]\label{prop:rhobound}
With identity restrictions, $0\le\rho\le 1$.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Lower bound: $\omega\ge0$ and $B\norm{X}_F^2>0$. Upper bound: by the Anderson--Morley
inequality \cite{AndersonMorley}, the largest eigenvalue of $L_\Kc$ satisfies
$\lambda_{\max}(L_\Kc)\le \max_{\{u,v\}\in E}(\deg u+\deg v)=B$. By
Proposition~\ref{prop:identity}, $\lambda_{\max}(L)=\lambda_{\max}(L_\Kc)\le B$, hence
$\omega=x^\top Lx\le\lambda_{\max}(L)\norm{x}^2\le B\norm{X}_F^2$, where
$\norm{x}=\norm{X}_F$ under the row-stacking identification. Dividing gives
$\rho\le1$.
\end{proof}

\subsection{The splitting \texorpdfstring{$\rho=\alpha\tilde\rho$}{rho = alpha rho-tilde} and the calibration window}\label{subsec:split}

\begin{intu}\Intu
$\rho$ silently answers two questions at once. Its numerator $\omega$ is \emph{blind}
to consensus---consensus is exactly $\ker L$---while its denominator
$\norm{X}_F^2$ counts consensus in full. So $\rho$ is ``disagreement divided by
(disagreement $+$ consensus),'' tangled with a second quantity saying which
\emph{shape} the disagreement takes. Untangling them costs one subtraction.
\end{intu}

\begin{proposition}[Exact factorisation]\label{prop:split}
Assume identity restrictions. Let $x=x_0\oplus x_\perp$ be the orthogonal
decomposition with $x_0$ the projection onto $\ker L$, and suppose $x_\perp\neq0$.
Then $\omega(x)=x_\perp^\top Lx_\perp$, $\norm{X}_F^2=\norm{x_0}^2+\norm{x_\perp}^2$, and
\begin{equation}\label{eq:split}
\boxed{\ \rho\;=\;\underbrace{\frac{\norm{x_\perp}^2}{\norm{X}_F^2}}_{\textstyle\alpha}
\cdot\underbrace{\frac{\omega}{B\norm{x_\perp}^2}}_{\textstyle\tilde\rho}\ }
\end{equation}
where $\alpha$ (the \emph{dissent fraction}) says \emph{how much} of the state is not
consensus, and $\tilde\rho$ (the \emph{mode index}) says \emph{which mode} the
disagreement occupies.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$Lx_0=0$ and $L$ symmetric give $x^\top Lx=x_\perp^\top Lx_\perp$, the cross terms
vanishing. The norm identity is Pythagoras. The factorisation is then multiplication
and division by $\norm{x_\perp}^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[The kernel is per-component, not the global mean]\label{rem:percomp}
For a connected $1$-skeleton $\ker L$ is the diagonal and $x_0=\mathbf1\otimes\bar x$.
For $b_0>1$ it is \emph{not}: $\ker(L_\Kc\otimes I_d)$ consists of the assignments
constant on \emph{each component}, so $x_0$ subtracts the \textbf{per-component}
mean. Using the global mean when $b_0>1$ would score between-component separation as
dissent when it in fact lies \emph{in} the kernel. This is a correctness condition,
and it is enforced in both implementations.
\end{remark}

\begin{proposition}[The achievable window]\label{prop:window}
$\tilde\rho=R_L(x_\perp)/B$ is a Rayleigh quotient of $L$ on $(\ker L)^\perp$, so by
Courant--Fischer
\begin{equation}\label{eq:window}
\tilde\rho\ \in\ \Big[\ \lambda_{\min}^{+}(L_{\Kc,\pi})/B,\ \ \lambda_{\max}(L_{\Kc,\pi})/B\ \Big],
\end{equation}
$\lambda_{\min}^{+}$ denoting the smallest \emph{nonzero} eigenvalue (the Fiedler value
when $\Kc$ is connected). Both endpoints are \textbf{graph invariants}, computable
from the coarse $1$-skeleton before any data is seen, and needing recomputation only
when \texttt{bind}/\texttt{collapse} changes the topology.
\end{proposition}

\begin{hon}\Hon
Three things, in order of importance.
\emph{(i)} \eqref{eq:split} resolves the non-monotonicity recorded in
Remark~\ref{rem:controller}: adding a well-aligned organ inflates $\norm{X}_F^2$ and
barely moves $\norm{x_\perp}^2$, so $\alpha$ dilutes while $\tilde\rho$ is untouched.
The controller should gate on $\tilde\rho$ and report $\alpha$ alongside.
\emph{(ii)} \eqref{eq:window} is \textbf{normalisation, not derivation}. Setting
$\varepsilon$ as a quantile $q$ of the window still requires choosing $q$; what changes
is that $\varepsilon$ is now expressed in units the graph supplies and adapts
automatically to rewiring. It is not a magic number eliminated, it is a magic number
made topology-relative, and it should not be sold as more than that.
\emph{(iii)} The split is exact \emph{only} for identity restrictions. With
non-identity maps $\ker L$ is not the per-component constants and no $n\times n$ matrix
carries $L$'s spectrum; both implementations then report $\alpha,\tilde\rho$ and the
window as undefined rather than approximate them. $\rho$ itself is unaffected.
\emph{(iv)} When $x_\perp=0$ exactly, $\alpha=0$ and $\tilde\rho=\bot$: perfect
consensus has no mode. Returning $0$ would assert ``the most global mode possible,''
a measurement that was not made.
\end{hon}

\begin{remark}[Measured, 2026-07-28]\label{rem:e1measured}
The claim that the dead dynamic range of $\rho$ is caused by encoder anisotropy
acting through $\alpha$ was tested and \textbf{does not survive}. The deployed encoder
returns unit-normalised vectors, for which $\alpha=(n-1)(1-\bar c)/n$ exactly, $\bar c$
the mean pairwise cosine. At the observed $\bar c\approx0.449$ with $n=7$ this gives
$\alpha\approx0.46$---not small. The compression of $\rho$ is produced jointly by
$\alpha$ and by $\tilde\rho$ sitting mid-window, with $B$ over-bounding
$\lambda_{\max}$ contributing further. Measured on a $7$-organ path complex,
$\tilde\rho\approx0.47$ inside a window $[0.146,0.854]$: \textbf{the mode index has
healthy dynamic range that $\rho$ was multiplying away.} See
\texttt{python/coherence.py} test 15.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Per-edge diagnostics and fragmentation]\label{rem:diag}
Because \eqref{eq:omega} is a \emph{sum over edges}, $\omega$ decomposes into
per-edge terms $\omega_e=\norm{(\delta x)_e}^2$. The \emph{worst edge}
$\arg\max_e\omega_e$ names the guilty coalition, so remediation can be \emph{aimed}
rather than global---provided $\omega>\text{tol}$ (below tolerance the argmax is
meaningless and is returned as $\bot$). Separately, the number of connected components
$b_0$ of the active $1$-skeleton is computed by union-find; $b_0>1$ flags that any
coherence claim is only \emph{local} to a component. Both are $O(|E|)$.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Spectral decomposition, when wanted]
Because $L$ is genuinely symmetric PSD, the spectral theorem applies exactly (not by
analogy): eigenpairs $(\lambda_i,\phi_i)$ of $L$ are honest \emph{modes of
disagreement}---small $\lambda$ are droppable noise, large $\lambda$ structural
conflict. This is the rigorous replacement for the earlier, unjustified ``cognitive
FFT.'' It is used only for diagnostics; the fast path \eqref{eq:rho} needs no
eigensolver.
\end{remark}

\section{The coarse-graining \texorpdfstring{$\pi_v$}{pi_v}: precision-weighted fusion}\label{sec:piv}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
An organ's single coarse belief-vector must summarise the many concepts living inside
it. Not all concepts are equally certain, so we do not average them equally: a
near-certain concept should dominate. The right notion of ``weight'' is
\emph{precision} (inverse variance), and the fusion is exactly how independent noisy
measurements of one quantity are optimally combined.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Concept as a Gaussian]\label{def:concept-gauss}
A concept is modelled as a Gaussian $\mathcal N(\mu_i,\Sigma_i)$ on $\Rd$ with a
low-rank-plus-isotropic covariance
\begin{equation}\label{eq:cov}
\Sigma_i \;=\; U_iU_i^{\top} + D_i\,I_d,\qquad U_i\in\R^{d\times k_i},\ D_i>0 .
\end{equation}
$\mu_i$ is the embedding; $U_iU_i^\top$ is anisotropic ``learned spread''; $D_i$ is an
isotropic noise floor (the epistemic humility of the concept).
\end{definition}

\begin{construction}[The coarse-graining map $\pi_v$]\label{con:piv}
For organ $v$ with active concepts $\{\mathcal N(\mu_i,\Sigma_i)\}_{i=1}^{n}$, the
coarse stalk $\pi_v\in\Fs(v)=\Rd$ is the maximum-likelihood fusion of the concepts
regarded as independent estimates of one latent ``what this organ believes'':
\begin{equation}\label{eq:piv}
\boxed{\ \pi_v \;=\;\Big(\textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Sigma_i^{-1}\Big)^{-1}
\Big(\textstyle\sum_{i=1}^{n}\Sigma_i^{-1}\mu_i\Big)\ }.
\end{equation}
Each concept pulls $\pi_v$ toward itself in proportion to its precision
$\Sigma_i^{-1}$. This is the $n$-way, order-independent, non-destructive
generalisation of the pairwise fusion of Section~\ref{sec:semantic}. If $v$ has no
active concept, $\pi_v=\bot$ (idle organs are excluded from $\rho$,
Remark~\ref{rem:embed}).
\end{construction}

\begin{proposition}[Why \eqref{eq:piv} is the fusion]
If $\theta$ is a latent vector and each concept is an independent observation
$\mu_i=\theta+\varepsilon_i$, $\varepsilon_i\sim\mathcal N(0,\Sigma_i)$, then the
log-likelihood is
$-\tfrac12\sum_i(\mu_i-\theta)^\top\Sigma_i^{-1}(\mu_i-\theta)+\mathrm{const}$; setting
its $\theta$-gradient to zero gives
$\big(\sum_i\Sigma_i^{-1}\big)\theta=\sum_i\Sigma_i^{-1}\mu_i$, i.e. \eqref{eq:piv}.
Order-independence is manifest (both sums are commutative). Non-destructiveness means
no concept is discarded: all contribute to both the precision sum and the mean.
\end{proposition}

\paragraph{The two computational paths (both exact).}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \textbf{Isotropic fast path.} When all $U_i$ are empty (the present reality of
  a freshly grown concept), $\Sigma_i^{-1}=D_i^{-1}I$ and \eqref{eq:piv} collapses to
  the scalar precision-weighted average
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:isopath}
  \pi_v=\frac{\sum_i D_i^{-1}\mu_i}{\sum_i D_i^{-1}},
  \end{equation}
  in $O(nd)$ with no matrix inversion.
  \item \textbf{General path.} With $U_i\neq\varnothing$, invert each
  $\Sigma_i$ by the Sherman--Morrison--Woodbury identity
  \begin{equation}\label{eq:woodbury}
  \Sigma_i^{-1}=D_i^{-1}I-D_i^{-1}U_i\big(I_{k_i}+D_i^{-1}U_i^\top U_i\big)^{-1}U_i^\top D_i^{-1},
  \end{equation}
  accumulate into a dense information matrix, and solve once: $O(nd k+d^3)$. A floor
  $D_i\ge 10^{-6}$ prevents a near-certain axiom from producing $1/D_i\approx10^{16}$
  and a numerical blow-up; it pins $\pi_v$ strongly but finitely.
\end{itemize}

\begin{remark}[Verified exactness]
The general path was checked numerically against a dense
$\big(\sum_i\Sigma_i^{-1}\big)^{-1}$ solve and agreed to $2.2\times10^{-16}$
(machine precision); equal-precision inputs reduce to the plain mean and a $9{:}1$
precision ratio pulls $\pi_v$ to the certain concept, as required. This is the
two-level glue: after execution, each organ's coarse stalk is \emph{overwritten} by
its own $\pi_v$, so coarse $\rho$ and fine geometry become one measurement at two
resolutions.
\end{remark}

\section{Semantic geometry: distance, fusion, entropy}\label{sec:semantic}
% =====================================================================

The concepts of Definition~\ref{def:concept-gauss} form a geometry of Gaussians. Three
computations act on it.

\subsection{Bures--Wasserstein distance between concepts}

\begin{intu}\Intu
How far apart are two \emph{uncertain} concepts? Not just their centres---their
spreads matter too. The $2$-Wasserstein (``optimal transport'') distance between
Gaussians answers this and is a true metric.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[$2$-Wasserstein between Gaussians]
\begin{equation}\label{eq:bures}
W_2^2\big(\mathcal N(\mu_1,\Sigma_1),\mathcal N(\mu_2,\Sigma_2)\big)
=\norm{\mu_1-\mu_2}^2+\Tr\Sigma_1+\Tr\Sigma_2
-2\,\Tr\!\Big[\big(\Sigma_1^{1/2}\Sigma_2\Sigma_1^{1/2}\big)^{1/2}\Big].
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Isotropic reduction to $O(k^3)$]\label{prop:buresiso}
If $\Sigma_1=D_1 I$ is isotropic and $\Sigma_2=U_2U_2^\top+D_2 I$ with
$U_2\in\R^{d\times k}$, then, writing $\{\sigma_j^2\}_{j=1}^k$ for the eigenvalues of
the $k\times k$ matrix $U_2^\top U_2$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:buresreduced}
W_2^2=\norm{\mu_1-\mu_2}^2+\big(\Tr\Sigma_1+\Tr\Sigma_2\big)
-2\sqrt{D_1}\Big[\textstyle\sum_{j=1}^k\sqrt{\sigma_j^2+D_2}+(d-k)\sqrt{D_2}\Big].
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
$\Sigma_1^{1/2}=\sqrt{D_1}\,I$, so
$\Sigma_1^{1/2}\Sigma_2\Sigma_1^{1/2}=D_1\Sigma_2$ and
$\big(D_1\Sigma_2\big)^{1/2}=\sqrt{D_1}\,\Sigma_2^{1/2}$, giving the cross term
$2\sqrt{D_1}\,\Tr\Sigma_2^{1/2}$. The eigenvalues of $\Sigma_2=U_2U_2^\top+D_2I$ are
$\sigma_j^2+D_2$ (on $\im U_2$, $j=1,\dots,k$) and $D_2$ (with multiplicity $d-k$ on
$\ker U_2^\top$); the nonzero eigenvalues of $U_2U_2^\top$ equal those of $U_2^\top
U_2$. Summing $\sqrt{\lambda}$ gives \eqref{eq:buresreduced}. Only a $k\times k$
eigendecomposition is needed.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}
This is exact for isotropic $\Sigma_1$, not a diagonal approximation. It is the
distance the engine uses to decide whether two stored concepts are near enough to
merge, and it feeds the acquisition/consolidation decisions of
Section~\ref{sec:growth}.
\end{remark}

\begin{construction}[The two terms are reported separately]\label{con:w2split}
$W_2^2$ is the sum of two quantities that are not commensurable, and the engine now
returns them apart rather than added:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:w2split}
W_2^2\;=\;\underbrace{\norm{\mu_1-\mu_2}^2}_{\text{semantic: \emph{what} they say}}
\;+\;\underbrace{\Tr\Sigma_1+\Tr\Sigma_2-2\Tr\big[(\Sigma_1^{1/2}\Sigma_2\Sigma_1^{1/2})^{1/2}\big]}_{\text{epistemic: \emph{how sure} they are}} .
\end{equation}
\end{construction}

\begin{proposition}[Degeneration and the scaling hazard]\label{prop:w2hazard}
Let both concepts be isotropic, $\Sigma_i=D_iI$, with $U_i$ empty. Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:w2iso}
W_2^2=\norm{\mu_1-\mu_2}^2+d\big(\sqrt{D_1}-\sqrt{D_2}\big)^2 .
\end{equation}
In particular $D_1=D_2\Rightarrow W_2^2=\norm{\mu_1-\mu_2}^2$ exactly.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Set $k=0$ in Proposition~\ref{prop:buresiso}: $\Tr\Sigma_1+\Tr\Sigma_2=d(D_1+D_2)$ and
the cross term is $2\sqrt{D_1}\cdot d\sqrt{D_2}$, so the covariance part is
$d(D_1+D_2-2\sqrt{D_1D_2})=d(\sqrt{D_1}-\sqrt{D_2})^2$.
\end{proof}

\begin{hon}\Hon
Proposition~\ref{prop:w2hazard} says two uncomfortable things.
\emph{(i) Today the machinery is inert.} Every grown concept carries the named prior
$D=1.0$ with empty $U$ (Remark~\ref{rem:uncal}), so $D_1=D_2$ and $W_2^2$ is
\textbf{exactly squared Euclidean distance}. The optimal-transport apparatus is
currently a Euclidean distance with a Wasserstein-shaped proof attached. Not wrong;
just not doing anything.
\emph{(ii) It becomes actively harmful the moment calibration is fixed.} The semantic
term is $d$-\emph{intensive}---bounded by $4$ for unit-normalised embeddings whatever
$d$ is---while the epistemic term is $d$-\emph{extensive}, growing linearly in $d$ for
a fixed disagreement about certainty. At $d=384$, the uncalibrated prior $D_1=1.0$
against a confident concept $c=0.95$ ($D_2=-\ln0.95$) gives an epistemic term of
$\approx230$ against a semantic term of $2$ for orthogonal embeddings: a ratio of
about $115$ to $1$, driven entirely by a self-reported confidence that
Remark~\ref{rem:uncal} identifies as unavailable and untrustworthy. Merge/split and
the consolidation logic of Section~\ref{sec:growth} would be governed by hallucinated
confidences.
This is a latent defect that switches on precisely when the system is
\emph{improved}. Summing a $d$-intensive and a $d$-extensive quantity and thresholding
the sum is a dimensional error, not a modelling preference; the two must be
thresholded separately. Implemented as \texttt{core::wasserstein\_2\_terms}
(2026-07-28), which also removes a byte-identical duplicate of this formula that had
been maintained in two files.
\end{hon}

\subsection{Pairwise fusion and its entropy}

\begin{definition}[Precision-weighted fusion]
The fusion of two concepts is the information-form product
\begin{equation}\label{eq:merge}
\Lambda_i=\Sigma_i^{-1},\qquad
\Sigma_\star=(\Lambda_1+\Lambda_2)^{-1},\qquad
\mu_\star=\Sigma_\star(\Lambda_1\mu_1+\Lambda_2\mu_2),
\end{equation}
i.e. \eqref{eq:piv} for $n=2$. Full covariances are fused via Woodbury
\eqref{eq:woodbury}, not element-wise.
\end{definition}

\begin{proposition}[Fused entropy by the matrix determinant lemma]
The differential entropy $h=\tfrac12\ln\!\big((2\pi e)^d\det\Sigma_\star\big)$ is
computed from a $k\times k$ determinant: for $\Sigma=UU^\top+DI$,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:mdl}
\det\Sigma=\det(DI+UU^\top)=D^{d}\,\det\!\big(I_k+D^{-1}U^\top U\big).
\end{equation}
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Matrix determinant lemma: $\det(A+UV^\top)=\det A\,\det(I+V^\top A^{-1}U)$ with
$A=DI$, $V=U$. Then $\det(DI)=D^d$ and $V^\top A^{-1}U=D^{-1}U^\top U$.
\end{proof}

\begin{remark}[Where a reported confidence belongs]\label{rem:uncal}
An earlier version of this engine set the noise floor $D$ of a \emph{grown} concept
from the generating model's self-reported confidence $c$ via $D=-\ln c$, falling back
to a named prior $D=1.0$ when $c$ was unavailable. \textbf{Both are now removed, and the
reason is dimensional.} The Bures term between two isotropic covariances is
$d(\sqrt{D_1}-\sqrt{D_2})^2$, which grows with $d$, whereas the semantic term
$\norm{\mu_1-\mu_2}^2\le 4$ regardless of $d$. At $d=384$ the pair $(D=1.0,\ c=0.95)$
gives an epistemic term of ${\approx}229.8$ against a semantic budget of $4$---a
$57{:}1$ ratio in favour of a self-reported number the architecture elsewhere declines
to trust. \emph{An isotropic variance in $d$ dimensions is the wrong destination for a
scalar confidence.}

$D$ is now the shared $O(1/d)$ shrinkage floor
$\Sigma_v = S_v/(n_{\mathrm{eff}}+\kappa) + [\kappa/(d(n_{\mathrm{eff}}+\kappa))+\varepsilon]I$,
a function of evidence rather than of assertion. A reported confidence instead enters
the \emph{Laplacian's} inner product, as the vertex weight $\nu_v$ at the bottom of the
cell-weight tower of Remark~\ref{rem:tower}, where it is dimensionally harmless and
actually load-bearing. An unreported confidence leaves $\nu_v=1$, meaning
\textsc{uncalibrated}---deliberately distinguishable from ``measured as average.''
The coherence score $\rho$ remains the primary signal.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[The cell-weight tower]\label{rem:tower}
Forman's cell weights are not decorative masses: they define the inner product in which
the combinatorial Laplacian is self-adjoint, which is the only reason a
Bochner--Weitzenb\"ock decomposition, and hence a Ricci term, exists at all. They are
therefore fixed by whichever Laplacian the engine uses, here $L=\delta^\top\Pi\delta$.
One rule generates every level:

\begin{quote}
the weight on a $p$-cell is the \textbf{harmonic mean} of the weights on its
$(p-1)$-faces.
\end{quote}

\[
  \nu_v \;=\; \text{reported confidence (floored)},\qquad
  \pi_e \;=\; \frac{2}{\nu_u^{-1}+\nu_v^{-1}},\qquad
  \tau_f \;=\; \frac{3}{\sum_{e\subset f}\pi_e^{-1}}.
\]

Each level is forced by error propagation: a $p$-cochain value is a signed sum over its
faces, the signs square away, so its variance is the sum of the face variances and its
precision is the harmonic sum. The per-face scaling---mean rather than sum---is the one
deliberate choice, taken because these weights appear only inside ratios
$w_{\text{face}}/w_{\text{cell}}$, so all levels must share a scale. It is also what
makes day-one degradation exact: all confidences $1$ gives every weight $1$, and the
augmented Forman curvature returns $4-\deg u-\deg v+3m$.

Two consequences worth stating. First, a harmonic mean is dominated by its
\emph{smallest} face, so one unreliable judgement discredits the pair or triple that
contains it---the correct behaviour for a joint measurement. Second,
$\pi_e$ is \textbf{not} the Hebbian coupling $w(\sigma,t)$: the coupling is a normalised
bind indicator on $[0,1]$, a precision is an inverse variance and is unbounded above,
and substituting one for the other computes the curvature of a different operator than
the one $\omega$ and $\rho$ are reported from.
\end{remark}

\section{The global section and the fine dynamics}\label{sec:fine}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
Inside an organ, reasoning is a \emph{flow}: new material perturbs the internal state,
and a gradient descent pulls it back toward internal coherence, subject to hard
constraints (axioms that must hold) and soft constraints (inferred, bendable).
\end{intu}

\begin{construction}[Fine state and deductive flow]\label{con:flow}
The fine state of an organ is a configuration $x\in(\Rd)^{m}$ over its $m$ active
concepts, with a scalar \emph{conflict functional} $\Phi(x)\ge0$ measuring internal
inconsistency (the fine-level analogue of $\omega$). Reasoning integrates the gradient
flow
\begin{equation}\label{eq:flow}
\dot x=-\nabla\Phi(x)
\end{equation}
by a fixed-step fourth-order Runge--Kutta (RK4) scheme. The reported
\emph{$\delta$-strain} of a step is the achieved change $\Delta\Phi$. Two constraint
types shape $\Phi$: \emph{rigid} constraints (user-stated axioms) act as hard
orthogonal projections onto an affine subspace via a projector $P$; \emph{fluid}
constraints (inferred facts) act as soft quadratic penalties.
\end{construction}

\begin{remark}[Observed sign behaviour]
On a real run, injecting a new concept raised $\Phi$ by a structural jump
($\delta\text{-strain}=+0.202$) and the subsequent RK4 flow pulled it back down
($\delta\text{-strain}=-0.081$): a structural expansion followed by a deductive
relaxation, exactly the intended dynamics.
\end{remark}

\begin{hon}\Hon
The precise closed form of $\Phi$ is an implementation-level choice (a penalised
quadratic on the fine complex) and is deliberately not over-specified here; what is
mathematically committed is \eqref{eq:flow}---a gradient flow on a nonnegative
conflict functional---and the rigid/fluid split as hard-projection vs soft-penalty.
Earlier drafts posited an infinite-dimensional Banach state and a Fréchet derivative
of the whole pipeline; both were \emph{removed} as unjustified---the pipeline runs
through discrete token sampling and database lookups and is nowhere differentiable
(see Section~\ref{sec:uncert}).
\end{hon}

\section{Cohomology and the diagnostic hierarchy}\label{sec:cohom}
% =====================================================================

\subsection{\texorpdfstring{$\Hzero$, $\Hone$}{H0, H1} and the \texorpdfstring{$b_1$}{b1} distinction}\label{subsec:cohom}

\begin{definition}[Sheaf cohomology, low degrees]
With coboundary $\delta:C^0\to C^1$ (and higher coboundaries on the full complex),
\[
\Hzero(\CC;\Fs)=\ker\delta^0\quad(\text{global sections}),\qquad
\Hone(\CC;\Fs)=\ker\delta^1/\im\delta^0\quad(\text{gluing failure}).
\]
\end{definition}

\begin{intu}\Intu
Two very different pathologies must never be confused. A \emph{structural hole}
($b_1\neq0$, a Betti number of the bare shape) means a \emph{binding is missing}---no
theorem yet ties some loop together. A \emph{gluing failure} ($\Hone\neq0$ of the
sheaf) means the data are \emph{contradictory}---the organs cannot be reconciled. The
first calls for \emph{growth}; the second calls for \emph{repair}. Treating a
contradiction as a hole would make you add structure when you should resolve a
conflict.
\end{intu}

\begin{remark}[$b_1$ is earned here]
$b_1$ is a defensible diagnostic \emph{only} because simplices were defined to mean
genuine $n$-ary binding (Remark~\ref{rem:nary}); a filled $2$-simplex really asserts a
joint relation, so an unfilled $1$-cycle really is a missing joint relation. This is
what most ``topological data analysis on knowledge graphs'' fails to justify.
\end{remark}

\subsection{The uncertainty stack}\label{sec:uncert}

\begin{intu}\Intu
Coherence is not correctness. Four instruments, ordered by cost, answer four different
questions; they are not interchangeable.
\end{intu}

\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.25}
\begin{tabular}{@{}p{3.1cm}p{5.4cm}p{2.6cm}p{2.7cm}@{}}
\hline
\textbf{Instrument} & \textbf{Question} & \textbf{Cost} & \textbf{When}\\
\hline
$\rho$ (coherence) & Are my parts agreeing? & $\approx$ free & every tick\\
$\hat\rho$ vs $\rho$ & Did I \emph{know} whether I'd agree? & free ($k$-NN) & once history exists\\
$b_0,\ b_1$ & What is structurally missing/broken? & medium & on conflict, active subcomplex\\
perturbation $J$ & Am I stable to paraphrase? & $k\times$ LLM calls & high-stakes only\\
\textsc{VerifyOp} & Am I actually \emph{right}? & medium & promotion gate\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

\begin{remark}[Perturbation is variance, not a derivative]
Stability is measured as \emph{paraphrase-perturbation variance}: run $k$ semantically
equivalent paraphrases and measure the spread of outcomes. This replaces an earlier
``Fréchet derivative $\norm{\partial F/\partial x}$'' that does not exist, because $F$
factors through discrete LLM sampling, a DAG generator, and database lookups---nothing
differentiable. The cost is $k\times$ API calls and must be rationed.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Expected precision, and the feedback edge]
$\hat\rho$ is a similarity-weighted $k$-NN prediction of $\rho$ from a log of past
$(\text{query embedding},\rho)$ pairs. The signed gap $\hat\rho-\rho$ separates
\emph{competence}, \emph{known ignorance} (``confidently I do not know this''), and
\emph{hallucination risk} (predicted agreement, observed disagreement). A
\textsc{VerifyOp} failure updates this store, so self-knowledge improves with use.
\end{remark}

\section{Dynamics: growth operators and the algebra \texorpdfstring{$\Dop$}{D}}\label{sec:growth}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
Learning \emph{is} change of shape. The complex binds organs that work together,
prunes idle ones, and---when a procedure repeatedly succeeds---promotes it to a new
first-class operator. Four axes of growth, one of which grows the very set of moves the
system can make.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Structural operators]
The base moves on $\CC$ are the typed operators
$\{\text{bind},\ \text{collapse},\ \text{split},\ \text{merge}\}$, each logged to an
immutable mutation history. (The earlier terminology ``Pachner move'' was discarded: a
knowledge complex is not a manifold, so the Pachner invariance guarantees do not
transfer.)
\end{definition}

\begin{construction}[The four growth axes]\label{con:axes}
Evolution proceeds along
\[
\underbrace{\delta \CC_{\text{fine}}}_{\text{concepts (semantic memory)}}\!,\quad
\underbrace{\delta \CC_{\text{coarse}}}_{\text{coalitions (functional connectivity)}}\!,\quad
\underbrace{\delta \Fs}_{\text{stalk/restriction data}}\!,\quad
\underbrace{\delta \Dop}_{\text{new operators (procedural skill)}} .
\]
The fourth axis is the essential one: the operator algebra is time-dependent,
$\Dop(t)\to\Dop(t+1)$.
\end{construction}

\begin{definition}[Hebbian coupling and decay]\label{def:hebb-decay}
For a coalition $\sigma$ with weight $w(\sigma,t)\in[0,1]$: co-activation increments
$w$ (``fire together $\Rightarrow$ wire together''); idle steps apply
$w(\sigma,t{+}1)=(1-\gamma)\,w(\sigma,t)$ for a decay rate $\gamma$, and a coalition
falling below a bind threshold \emph{collapses} (garbage collection). A pair past the
bind threshold is a $1$-simplex (edge) of $\Kc$.
\end{definition}

\begin{construction}[Consolidation via persistence, and microneurons]\label{con:micro}
Over the weight filtration, \emph{persistent homology} reads which coalitions and
concept-cycles \emph{survive}; survivors are promoted into permanent structure. In
particular a \emph{microneuron} is a promoted operadic sub-tree: when a composite DAG
repeatedly resolves conflict \emph{and} passes verification, it is named and
thereafter emitted by the planner as a single atomic generator of $\Dop$---this is
$\delta\Dop$ made concrete. The promotion gate is \textsc{VerifyOp}
(Section~\ref{sec:verify}): a composite becomes a generator only if it survives the
external oracle.
\end{construction}

\section{The operad and parallel scheduling}\label{sec:operad}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
A reasoning act is a small program: a directed acyclic graph (DAG) of operators, some
depending on others. Independent operators can run at once; the scheduler slices the
DAG into layers of mutually independent work sized to the real machine.
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Operadic foliation]\label{def:foliation-note}
A reasoning act is a DAG $T=(N,\prec)$ of operator nodes. The scheduler foliates $T$
into the antichain layers of the partial order $\prec$: layer $0$ is the set of
minimal nodes (in-degree $0$), and layer $\ell+1$ is the set of nodes all of whose
predecessors lie in layers $\le\ell$. Each layer is a \emph{maximal commuting slice}
executed in parallel; correctness follows because nodes in one antichain share no
$\prec$-dependency. Node ``support'' sets index which fine-complex vertices an operator
reads/writes, so slices with disjoint support are safe to co-execute.
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[``Operad'' is not earned; the intended structure is a trace monoid]\label{rem:tracemonoid}
What Definition~\ref{def:foliation-note} describes is antichain layering of a DAG. An
operad requires multi-input composition together with equivariance, associativity and
unit axioms, none of which are stated or used here. The structure the design is
reaching for is a \emph{trace monoid} $\mathbb M(\Sigma,I)$ (Mazurkiewicz): the free
monoid on the operator alphabet modulo $ab=ba$ for pairs in an \emph{independence
relation} $I$, whose canonical Foata normal form is exactly the antichain layering the
scheduler computes. That identification would be a sharpening rather than a downgrade,
since it supplies normal forms, a Hilbert series and a canonical form for
deduplication. The terminology should be corrected regardless.
\end{remark}

\begin{hon}\Hon
\textbf{The trace-monoid identification does not currently hold, and the reason is
three separate defects.} Measured 2026-07-29 against the deployed operator set
(\texttt{python/independence.py}, reading the declared supports out of
\texttt{primitives.cpp}):
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\roman*),nosep]
\item \emph{The relation is not symmetric.} In the scheduler, admitting \emph{any}
  empty-support node---including a read-only one---marks the whole slice as a global
  mutation and blocks every later node. So $8$ of the $15$ operator pairs can share a
  slice in one order and not the other. An independence relation is symmetric by
  definition; a non-symmetric relation defines no trace monoid, hence no Foata normal
  form and no Hilbert series. This is very likely an unintended scheduling behaviour
  and is worth fixing on its own merits.
\item \emph{The support sets are placeholders.} Every \texttt{get\_support()} returns a
  hardcoded literal ($\varnothing$, $\{0\}$ or $\{1\}$), three of the six operators
  returning the same $\{0\}$, with a comment standing in for an analysis of what the
  operator actually touches. Any invariant computed from them describes six constants,
  not the system.
\item \emph{The convention for $\varnothing$ is inverted.} In the engine an empty
  support means \textsc{global} (touches everything, must run isolated); read as a
  set, $\varnothing$ is disjoint from everything and would mean \emph{independent of
  everything}. The two readings give capacities differing by a factor of $1.8$
  ($\kappa=3.00$ versus $\kappa=5.45$ against $\kappa=6$ for the free monoid), so the
  quantity is not well defined until the convention is fixed.
\end{enumerate}
Under the reading the engine actually implements the algebra is \emph{nearly free}
($9\%$ below the free monoid), because three of six operators share one support. The
order of repair is: fix the symmetry defect; derive real read/write sets---including
the shared state the current model ignores entirely (the store, the obstruction counter
$\Omega$, the mutation log, and the coarse stalks that $\pi_v$ overwrites after every
execution); only then recompute $I$. Operators that all mutate $\Omega$ do not commute
whatever their vertex supports say. Until that is done, no capacity, Koszulity or
resolution claim about $\Dop$ should appear in this document.
\end{hon}

\section{The lift--project adjunction \texorpdfstring{$\mathcal L\dashv\mathcal M$}{L -| M}}\label{sec:adjunction}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
Two worlds: free-form natural language, and structured reasoning DAGs. One map
\emph{lifts} language into a validated DAG; a partner map \emph{projects} a DAG back
into language. Meaning that carries no reasoning content collapses under the lift---it
lands in the kernel.
\end{intu}

\begin{construction}[The boundary morphisms]
Let $\pi$ (``lift'', $\mathcal L$) send a natural-language request to a validated
reasoning DAG, and $G$ (``project'', $\mathcal M$) send a DAG to a natural-language
rendering. Empirically, semantically void input maps into $\ker\pi$: a joke embedded
in a mathematical prompt produced \emph{zero} DAG nodes, exactly as a kernel element
should. The lift is guarded by a validator that rejects malformed DAGs at the boundary
(duplicate ids, dangling children, cycles by DFS colouring, absence of a terminal
output node, disconnection), so the downstream engine only ever receives well-formed
programs.
\end{construction}

\begin{hon}\Hon
$\mathcal L\dashv\mathcal M$ is the intended categorical \emph{framing}
(lift left-adjoint to project) and it organises the design usefully; the unit/counit
triangle identities are \emph{not} formally verified here, so ``adjunction'' should be
read as a structuring principle plus concrete morphisms $(\pi,G)$ with the observed
$\ker\pi$ behaviour---not as a proved theorem. Stating this is part of the honesty
invariant.
\end{hon}

\section{Verification: the external oracle}\label{sec:verify}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
Coherence governs whether to keep exploring or stop and reconcile; only an external
checker touches \emph{truth}. And ``I could not check'' must never be mistaken for
``it is false.''
\end{intu}

\begin{definition}[Three-valued verification]
\textsc{VerifyOp} calls a sandboxed symbolic oracle returning one of three verdicts,
which act on an obstruction counter $\Omega$:
\begin{center}
\renewcommand{\arraystretch}{1.2}
\begin{tabular}{@{}lll@{}}
\hline
exit & verdict & effect on state\\
\hline
$0$ & \textsc{Verified} & obstruction cleared; state may crystallise\\
$1$ & \textsc{Refuted} & $\Omega\mathrel{+}=1000$; forced into \textsc{Resolve}\\
$2$ & \textsc{Unverifiable} & $\Omega$ \emph{untouched}\\
\hline
\end{tabular}
\end{center}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Absence of proof is not proof of absence]
An earlier version treated every nonzero exit as failure, conflating \textsc{Refuted}
and \textsc{Unverifiable}; that would spike conflict over the oracle's own blind spots
and lock the system into \textsc{Resolve} over perfectly good mathematics. A checker
crash also returns \textsc{Unverifiable}. This three-valued logic is the promotion
gate of Construction~\ref{con:micro}.
\end{remark}

\section{The controller}\label{sec:controller}
% =====================================================================

\begin{definition}[Coherence-gated mode]
With threshold $\varepsilon$, the controller selects
\begin{equation}
\text{mode}(x)=
\begin{cases}
\textsc{Resolve}, & \rho>\varepsilon\ \text{(reconcile the guilty edge before expanding)},\\
\textsc{Explore}, & \rho\le\varepsilon,\\
\textsc{Unknown}, & \rho=\bot\ \text{(no measurement)}.
\end{cases}
\end{equation}
\end{definition}

\begin{remark}[Three modes, and empirical $\varepsilon$]\label{rem:controller}
The \textsc{Unknown} branch is essential: when $\rho$ is undefined the controller
\emph{refuses} to default to \textsc{Explore}, because ``no measurement'' is not
``all is well.'' The threshold is calibrated, not guessed: on real $384$-dimensional
embeddings, agreement gave $\rho\approx0.00$, related-but-distinct $\approx0.06$, and
off-topic $\approx0.14$, so $\varepsilon\approx0.10$ is a principled starting gate.
Two cautions from live runs: $\rho$ is \emph{not} monotonic in the number of active
organs (adding a well-aligned organ raises the normaliser faster than the discord, so
$\rho$ dilutes), and comparisons across differently-sized complexes must be made with
care.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[Superseded: gate on $\tilde\rho$, not $\rho$]\label{rem:gate-tilde}
Both cautions above are consequences of a single fact, isolated in
Proposition~\ref{prop:split}: $\rho=\alpha\tilde\rho$, and \emph{only $\alpha$ has the
pathologies}. Organ-count dilution moves $\alpha$ and leaves $\tilde\rho$ invariant;
cross-complex comparison is unsafe for $\rho$ because $B$ and $\norm{X}_F^2$ both
change, but $\tilde\rho$ is comparable across complexes once expressed as its position
inside the window \eqref{eq:window}. The controller should therefore gate on
$\tilde\rho$---or on its window position---and report $\alpha$ alongside as the
answer to a genuinely different question. The typed reading is what the single number
could not give: large $\alpha$ with small $\tilde\rho$ is a two-camp split wanting
\texttt{split}/\texttt{bind}; small $\alpha$ with large $\tilde\rho$ is one outlier
organ wanting edge repair.
\end{remark}

\section{What is and is not claimed}\label{sec:limits}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
The topology's deepest gift is not that it makes the system smart, but that it makes
the claim of augmentation \emph{falsifiable}: memory, consistency, and growth become
operations on \emph{one} object, with cheap provable guarantees and a live measure of
whether augmentation is actually happening.
\end{intu}

\paragraph{Diagnostic vs.\ constitutive use.}
A structure can \emph{measure} a process happening elsewhere (diagnostic) or \emph{be
part of} the computation (constitutive). Today the sheaf is used diagnostically: the
LLM reasons, $\rho$ grades coherence. Four identified moves promote it toward
constitutive use, none requiring a smarter LLM:
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\arabic*),nosep]
  \item \emph{sheaf diffusion as inference}: running $\dot x=-Lx$ to equilibrium
        converges to the orthogonal projection onto $\ker L=\Hzero$---it
        \emph{constructs} the least-discord global section, a reconciled state no
        single organ held (with identity restrictions this is consensus/averaging; its
        power grows with non-trivial maps);
  \item \emph{non-trivial (learned) restriction maps}, the formal home of ``quality of
        a connection'' (cf.\ Prop.~\ref{prop:identity} box);
  \item \emph{higher-cell Laplacians}, propagating joint constraints pairwise
        message-passing cannot represent;
  \item \emph{persistence as structural learning} (Construction~\ref{con:micro}).
\end{enumerate}

\paragraph{The creativity boundary (Boden).}
With frozen LLM weights the system realises \emph{combinatorial} and \emph{exploratory}
creativity---novel recombination, and search within a fixed representational
space---at full strength. \emph{Transformational} creativity (enlarging the space
itself) is largely out of reach, with one genuine sliver: each promoted operator
($\delta\Dop$) enlarges the system's \emph{effective} action space. So universal
adaptivity is a claim about \emph{organisation}, bounded by the model's expressible
\emph{content}.

\begin{hon}\Hon
\textbf{Bottom line.} The simplices do not hand you augmented intelligence. They hand
you a rigorous, measurable, self-checking substrate on which
augmentation-via-structure \emph{might} emerge---through measured cross-organ
error-correction and accumulation---and on which you can \emph{tell whether it is
emerging rather than deceiving yourself}. Whether the bet wins (whole $>$ sum) is
unknown, and no elegance of topology guarantees it. The instrumentation is what makes
it a real research bet rather than a beautiful story.
\end{hon}

\subsection{Predictive coding is precision-weighted sheaf diffusion}

\begin{proposition}[The identity]\label{prop:pc}
Let each edge carry a scalar precision $\pi_e>0$ (the confidence attached to that
organ pair's agreement). Define the \emph{predictive-coding free energy}
\begin{equation}\label{eq:pcfreeenergy}
\mathcal F(x)\;=\;\sum_{e=\{u,v\}}\pi_e\,\norm{\varepsilon_e}^2,
\qquad \varepsilon_e=(\delta x)_e=\rest{u\triang e}(x_u)-\rest{v\triang e}(x_v).
\end{equation}
Then $\mathcal F$ is exactly the discord $\omega$ of Definition~\ref{def:laplacian}
with edge weights $\pi_e$: $\mathcal F(x)=x^\top L_\pi x$ for the precision-weighted
Laplacian $L_\pi=\delta^\top \Pi\, \delta$, $\Pi=\diag(\pi_e)$. \emph{Inference}
(settling on a reconciled belief) is the gradient flow $\dot x=-\nabla\mathcal
F(x)=-L_\pi x$, i.e. sheaf diffusion (item (1) of Section~\ref{sec:limits}) with a
precision-weighted Laplacian in place of the unweighted one.
\end{proposition}

\begin{proposition}[Weighted Anderson--Morley]\label{prop:wam}
Let $\pi_e\ge0$, $\Pi=\diag(\pi_e)$, $L_\pi=\delta^\top\Pi\delta$, and let the
\emph{weighted degree} be $d_\pi(v)=\sum_{e\ni v}\pi_e$. Assume every restriction map
is a contraction, $\norm{\rest{v\triang e}}\le1$ (orthogonality suffices, and identity
is the special case). Then
\begin{equation}\label{eq:wam}
\lambda_{\max}(L_\pi)\ \le\ B_\pi\ :=\ \max_{e=\{u,v\}\in E}\big(d_\pi(u)+d_\pi(v)\big),
\end{equation}
so $\rho=\omega/(B_\pi\norm{X}_F^2)\in[0,1]$ as before.
\end{proposition}
\begin{proof}
Write $y_v=\norm{x_v}$. By the triangle inequality and the contraction hypothesis,
$\norm{\rest{u\triang e}(x_u)-\rest{v\triang e}(x_v)}\le\norm{x_u}+\norm{x_v}$, so
\[
x^\top L_\pi x=\sum_e\pi_e\norm{(\delta x)_e}^2\le\sum_e\pi_e(y_u+y_v)^2=y^\top Q_\pi y,
\qquad Q_\pi:=B\Pi B^\top,
\]
$B$ the \emph{unsigned} incidence matrix, so $Q_\pi=D_\pi+A_\pi$ is the weighted
signless Laplacian. Since $\norm{y}=\norm{X}_F$ it suffices to bound
$\lambda_{\max}(Q_\pi)$. The nonzero spectrum of $B\Pi B^\top$ equals that of
$M:=\Pi^{1/2}B^\top B\,\Pi^{1/2}$, which is nonnegative symmetric with $M_{ee}=2\pi_e$
and $M_{ee'}=\sqrt{\pi_e\pi_{e'}}$ when $e\neq e'$ share a vertex. Apply the
Collatz--Wielandt bound $\lambda_{\max}(M)\le\max_e s_e^{-1}\sum_{e'}M_{ee'}s_{e'}$
with the strictly positive test vector $s_e=\sqrt{\pi_e}$ (deleting zero-weight edges
first):
\[
\frac1{\sqrt{\pi_e}}\Big(2\pi_e\sqrt{\pi_e}+\sum_{e'\sim e}\sqrt{\pi_e\pi_{e'}}\sqrt{\pi_{e'}}\Big)
=2\pi_e+\sum_{e'\sim e}\pi_{e'}=d_\pi(u)+d_\pi(v),
\]
using $\sum_{e'\sim e}\pi_{e'}=(d_\pi(u)-\pi_e)+(d_\pi(v)-\pi_e)$. Maximising over $e$
gives \eqref{eq:wam}.
\end{proof}

\begin{hon}\Hon
Two points of discipline about Proposition~\ref{prop:wam}.
\emph{(i) The contraction hypothesis is load-bearing and was previously implicit.}
Without $\norm{\rest{v\triang e}}\le1$ the first inequality in the proof fails and the
bound is simply false; a caller supplying non-orthogonal learned maps can drive
$\rho>1$. Both implementations detect this and warn on \texttt{stderr} rather than
silently clamping and hiding it. Any restatement of this proposition that drops the
hypothesis is wrong, and stating $L_\pi=\delta^\top\Pi\delta$ in full sheaf generality
while proving it only for identity restrictions is the specific error being corrected
here.
\emph{(ii) No novelty is claimed.} Weighted bounds of this shape are standard spectral
graph theory via the signless Laplacian; the proof is given in full only because the
exact constant is needed and because $\pi\equiv1$ must provably recover the classical
$\max(\deg u+\deg v)$ for the parity tests to mean anything.
\end{hon}

\begin{remark}[The Hebbian weights now reach the Laplacian]\label{rem:hebbwired}
Definition~\ref{def:hebb-decay} gives every coalition a weight $w(\sigma,t)\in[0,1]$,
but the unweighted \eqref{eq:omega} could not see it: the functional-connectivity
growth axis was invisible to the only quantity computed every tick. Setting
$\pi_e:=w(\sigma_e,t)$ closes that gap at zero cost---$B_\pi$ is $O(|E|)$ exactly as
$B$ was, and no eigensolver is introduced. This is implemented and off by default.
\end{remark}

\begin{remark}[This is the fine-level flow, one level up]
Construction~\ref{con:flow}'s RK4 descent $\dot x=-\nabla\Phi(x)$ already realises
predictive-coding inference \emph{inside} an organ's fine complex. Proposition
\ref{prop:pc} proposes the same dynamics at the \emph{coarse} level, on $L_\pi$,
closing the diagnostic/constitutive gap identified in Section~\ref{sec:limits}.
\end{remark}

\subsection{Learning the restriction maps at zero training cost}

\begin{hon}\Hon
Section~\ref{sec:limits} flagged non-trivial restriction maps as the formal home of
``connection quality,'' with the open question: learned from what, at zero budget,
with no backpropagation infrastructure? Predictive coding answers this with a purely
local rule.
\end{hon}

\begin{construction}[Oja-stabilised Hebbian update]\label{con:hebb}
Treat each restriction map $R_{v,e}:=\rest{v\triang e}$ as a learnable matrix. The
predictive-coding gradient is local to the edge:
\begin{equation}\label{eq:hebb}
\frac{\partial \mathcal F}{\partial R_{u,e}} = 2\pi_e\,\varepsilon_e\,x_u^{\top},
\qquad
\Delta R_{u,e} \;=\; -\eta\,\pi_e\,\varepsilon_e\,x_u^{\top}
\end{equation}
for a step size $\eta>0$ — a rank-one Hebbian update using only quantities already
local to organ $u$ and edge $e$. Raw Hebbian growth is unbounded, so an Oja
normalisation term $-\eta\,\norm{\varepsilon_e}^2 R_{u,e}$ is added to keep
$R_{u,e}$ bounded, exactly as in Oja's rule for Hebbian PCA. No backpropagation
graph, no global loss: each edge updates from its own residual.
\end{construction}

\subsection{Criticality: a self-tuned threshold instead of a guessed one}

\begin{intu}\Intu
The coherence threshold $\varepsilon\approx0.10$ (Section~\ref{sec:controller}) was
fit empirically to a handful of observations. Criticality theory offers a
principled replacement: tune the system to sit where a single perturbation triggers,
on average, exactly one further event.
\end{intu}

\begin{construction}[Branching-ratio setpoint]
Define the branching ratio $\sigma$ as the mean number of new binds triggered per
co-activation event. Following Beggs--Plenz neuronal-avalanche theory and the
Kinouchi--Copelli dynamic-range result, $\sigma\to1$ (criticality) maximises the
system's dynamic range and marks the transition between over-damped (nothing
restructures) and runaway (everything restructures) regimes. \textbf{Proposed:}
replace the fixed $\varepsilon$ with a controller that adjusts it to hold
$\sigma\approx1$, measured where sample size is large enough to mean anything (the
fine level; the six-organ coarse level uses an empirical-quantile $\varepsilon$
meanwhile).
\end{construction}

\subsection{Homeostatic growth trigger}

\begin{construction}[Deviation-from-setpoint trigger]
Following Butz--van Ooyen structural plasticity, let $a(t)$ be a leaky-integrated
activity/coherence signal with target $a^\ast$; growth (attachment,
Section~\ref{con:micro}) is triggered by \emph{integrated} deviation,
\begin{equation}
\dot z = \nu\big(a^\ast-a(t)\big),
\end{equation}
gating attachment only once $z$ crosses a bound. This is thrash-resistant by
construction: a single transient coherence spike does not trigger growth, only a
sustained deviation does — replacing the fixed decay constant of
Section~\ref{sec:growth} with an integrator.
\end{construction}

\subsection{Selectionism: variation, verification, promotion}

\begin{construction}[Attachment as selection]
When diffusion (Proposition~\ref{prop:pc}) fails to reduce $\mathcal F$ below
tolerance, generate $k$ candidate attachments at the located obstruction (the
worst edge of Remark~\ref{rem:diag}, or its cohomological generalisation, the
harmonic representative of a persisting $[\zeta]\in\Hone$); each candidate is an
Edelman-style \emph{variant}. \textsc{VerifyOp} (Section~\ref{sec:verify}) is the
\emph{sole} selection criterion: a variant is promoted to $\Dop$
(Construction~\ref{con:micro}) only if it survives verification. Coherence
improvement $\Delta\rho$ alone must never promote a variant — a fluent but false
reconciliation is exactly the failure mode this excludes.
\end{construction}

\begin{hon}\Hon
\textbf{What this layer does and does not buy, stated plainly.}
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
\item It does \emph{not} raise the LLM's per-call reasoning quality; the $k$
  attachment samples are \emph{aimed and rationed} by the located obstruction, not a
  smarter model.
\item Diffusion reconciles existing information; it never manufactures a true fact.
  \textsc{VerifyOp} remains the sole arbiter of truth, unconditionally.
\item All four mechanisms are \emph{slope} mechanisms: on day one, with
  $\pi_e\equiv1$, fixed $\varepsilon$, a reactive (non-integrated) trigger, and
  $k=1$, the system degrades exactly to the architecture described in
  Sections~\ref{sec:complex}--\ref{sec:controller}. The bet, as always, is on
  accumulation, not on an improved first day.
\item Criticality is used here as an \emph{engineering} target (maximise dynamic
  range), not asserted as a governing law of cognition; it is meaningful only at
  sample sizes where a branching ratio is statistically defined.
\end{itemize}
The one-sentence summary: promote the coherence measure from a \emph{scorer} to a
\emph{search controller} — detect struggle as a persisting obstruction, read the
obstruction as the address of the needed structure, spend the scarce LLM sample
exactly there, verify, and promote survivors, with the same rule applying
self-similarly at every level of the stratified complex.
\end{hon}

\section{The driving application: a thermal Hodge spectrum on \texorpdfstring{$\mathbb{CP}^3$}{CP3}}\label{app:cp3}
% =====================================================================

\begin{intu}\Intu
The architecture is aimed at a concrete open problem, so the mathematics has a target,
not just a form.
\end{intu}

The target is the spectrum and time-evolution of a \emph{thermally-weighted}
Hodge--de~Rham Laplacian $\Delta_\beta$ on $1$-forms over complex projective space
$\mathbb{CP}^3$ with the Fubini--Study metric, where the weight $e^{-\beta E}$ is
supplied by a two-qubit Hamiltonian $K$. Two halves:
\begin{itemize}[nosep]
  \item \emph{Retrievable core.} The \emph{unweighted} Hodge spectrum on
  $\mathbb{CP}^n$ is classical (Ikeda--Taniguchi via $SU(n{+}1)$ representation theory
  \cite{IkedaTaniguchi}); the weighted operator $\Delta_\beta$ is a Witten /
  Bakry--Émery drift-Laplacian, a known deformation.
  \item \emph{Novel frontier.} The thermally-weighted, symmetry-broken spectrum is a
  genuine new step and carries real confident-error risk.
\end{itemize}
The role of \textsc{tatiana} on this problem is not to out-reason a frontier model in
one shot but to \emph{run the loop}: retrieve the classical core, grow concept-nodes,
register a coherence spike at the frontier, verify what it can, and produce a grounded,
honestly-hedged attempt---measuring its own augmentation as it goes. This is precisely
the setting where the diagnostic hierarchy of Section~\ref{sec:uncert} earns its keep:
a lone LLM answer to the frontier half is confidently unfalsifiable; here the
disagreement is measured and localised.
