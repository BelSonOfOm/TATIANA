\begin{mosbox}{13. Coherence-Gated Controller}

\BodyFont

The controller determines the system's operating mode using the
normalized coherence measure

\[
\rho.
\]

Rather than continuously expanding the knowledge complex, MOS first asks
whether the current mathematical state is sufficiently coherent to
justify further exploration.

This decision is governed by the coherence threshold

\[
\varepsilon.
\]

\end{mosbox}

%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%
%% HERO EQUATION
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{eqbox}

\[
\boxed{
\operatorname{mode}(x)=
\begin{cases}
\textbf{Resolve},
&
\rho>\varepsilon,
\\[3mm]
\textbf{Explore},
&
\rho\le\varepsilon,
\\[3mm]
\textbf{Unknown},
&
\rho=\bot.
\end{cases}
}
\]

\end{eqbox}

%% MODES
%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%%

\begin{highlightbox}

\SectionTitle{Controller Modes}

\BodyFont

\textbf{Resolve}

High discord.
Reconcile conflicting structures before further expansion.

\vspace{2mm}

\textbf{Explore}

Coherence remains acceptable.
Additional reasoning and structural growth may proceed.

\vspace{2mm}

\textbf{Unknown}

No coherence measurement is available.

Importantly,

\[
\boxed{
\text{Unknown}
\neq
\text{Explore}.
}
\]

Absence of measurement is not interpreted as evidence of consistency.

\end{highlightbox}

\begin{mosbox}{Empirical Threshold}

\BodyFont

The manuscript reports representative coherence values obtained from
384-dimensional embeddings:

\[
\begin{aligned}
\text{Agreement}
&\approx
0.00,
\\[2mm]
\text{Related Concepts}
&\approx
0.06,
\\[2mm]
\text{Off-Topic}
&\approx
0.14.
\end{aligned}
\]

These observations motivate

\[
\boxed{
\varepsilon\approx0.10
}
\]

as an initial operating threshold.

Two cautions remain important.

\begin{itemize}

\item
The normalized discord is not necessarily monotonic as the number of
active organs increases.

\item
Comparisons across differently-sized complexes require additional care.

\end{itemize}

\end{mosbox}

\begin{mosbox}{{14. Claims, Non-Claims and Future Directions}}

\BodyFont

MOS should be interpreted as a mathematical framework for structured
knowledge organization rather than a completed intelligent system.

The present work establishes the mathematical architecture and introduces
tools for measuring coherence, structural growth and verification.

Several directions identified in the manuscript remain future work.

\end{mosbox}

\begin{highlightbox}

\SectionTitle{Toward Constitutive Reasoning}

\BodyFont

The manuscript identifies four developments that would move the sheaf
from a diagnostic role toward a constitutive computational role.

\begin{enumerate}

\item
Sheaf diffusion as inference

\[
\dot{x}
=
-Lx
\]

converging toward the least-discord global section.

\vspace{2mm}

\item

Non-trivial learned restriction maps.

\vspace{2mm}

\item

Higher-cell Laplacians for higher-order constraint propagation.

\vspace{2mm}

\item

Persistence-driven structural learning through the growth mechanism
introduced in Section~9.

\end{enumerate}

\end{highlightbox}

\begin{mosbox}{Creativity Boundary}

\BodyFont

Following the discussion in the manuscript, MOS presently supports
combinatorial and exploratory creativity through structured
recombination within a fixed representational space.

Transformational creativity remains limited.

One genuine avenue for expansion is the growth operator

\[
\delta\mathcal D,
\]

through which verified procedures become new primitive operators,
expanding the effective action space of the system.

\end{mosbox}
