\begin{mosbox}{12. Verification: The External Oracle}

\BodyFont

Internal coherence and external truth are distinct concepts.

The sheaf coherence measure determines whether the system should continue
exploring or reconcile conflicting information, but it does not certify
mathematical correctness.

Truth is determined exclusively by an external symbolic verification
oracle.

\[
\boxed{
\text{Coherence}
\neq
\text{Truth}
}
\]

Verification therefore acts as the final gate before new mathematical
knowledge is permanently incorporated into the system.

\end{mosbox}

\hline

\textbf{Exit Code}
&
\textbf{Verdict}
&
\textbf{Effect}

\\

\hline

\color{green!70!black}\textbf{0}

Verified

Obstruction is cleared.

\[
\Omega

\textbf{Resolve} mode.

\\

\color{orange!90!black}\textbf{2}

&
Unverifiable

&
No change is made to the obstruction counter.

The computation remains unresolved.

\\

\hline

\end{tabular}

\end{center}

\end{mosbox}

\begin{highlightbox}

\SectionTitle{Absence of Proof Is Not Proof of Absence}

\BodyFont

Earlier versions of MOS treated every nonzero exit code as a failure.

This incorrectly identified

\[
\boxed{
\text{Refuted}
\equiv
\text{Unverifiable},
}
\]

causing unresolved computations and oracle failures to be interpreted as
mathematical contradictions.

The present framework explicitly separates these outcomes.

An oracle crash likewise returns
\textbf{Unverifiable}.

\end{highlightbox}

\begin{remarkbox}

\BodyFont

\textbf{Central Principle}

MOS separates three fundamentally different notions:

\[
\boxed{
\text{Coherence}
\neq
\text{Verification}
\neq
\text{Truth}.
}
\]

Coherence measures internal compatibility.

Verification evaluates mathematical correctness.

Truth is never inferred solely from structural consistency.

\end{remarkbox}
